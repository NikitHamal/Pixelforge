## Animation state vocabulary

Consumers drive animation by state name, so the vocabulary is shared across packs.
The most common state names:

```
hurt (87)  ·  death (87)  ·  idle_down (52)  ·  idle_side (52)  ·  walk_down (52)  ·  walk_side (52)  ·  idle_up (51)  ·  walk_up (51)  ·  attack_side (48)  ·  run_side (46)  ·  attack_up (45)  ·  ready_down (45)  ·  ready_side (45)  ·  ready_up (45)  ·  parry_down (45)  ·  parry_side (45)  ·  parry_up (45)  ·  evade_down (45)  ·  evade_side (45)  ·  evade_up (45)  ·  run_down (44)  ·  run_up (44)  ·  attack_down (44)  ·  idle (30)  ·  cast (21)  ·  cast_side (18)  ·  walk (12)  ·  tiles (12)  ·  attack (9)  ·  block (7)  ·  lunge (5)  ·  idle_downright (5)  ·  idle_upright (5)  ·  idle_upleft (5)  ·  idle_left (5)  ·  idle_downleft (5)  ·  walk_downright (5)  ·  walk_upright (5)  ·  walk_upleft (5)  ·  walk_left (5)
```

Locomotion is `idle_*`, `walk_*`, `run_*` (`_down` / `_side` / `_up`, or `_e`/`_se`/… on the 8-facing RTS packs).
Combat is `attack_*`, `bow_side`, `cast`, `cast_side`, `block`, `ready_*`, `parry_*`, `evade_*`.
Reaction is `hurt` and `death` (`loop: false`).
