Nik1 on-device models

Three specialists, running in this page. No server, no GPU, no network calls after load — the weights are 430 KB in total and they answer in milliseconds. Same code path as the Node benchmark.

1 · Router — utterance → tool call

A 248k-parameter decoder proposes the call; the grammar and the schema validator decide whether it is allowed. Invalid output cannot escape: it is repaired, or a deterministic router answers instead.

A 132k-parameter bi-encoder embeds your words, then scores a precomputed table of the catalogue. 81% of held-out briefs put an acceptable asset first.

3 · Palette — sprite → colour budget

5 KB MLP over 41 hand-computable features. Draw on the grid; it tells you which palette family the sprite belongs to — useful when importing art of unknown provenance.
